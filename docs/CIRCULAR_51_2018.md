# THE SYMBOL LEGEND

This is the part that cannot be read from plain text, and it is the whole
grammar of the document. A bare number is hours for the whole class. Every
other mark means something different.

| symbol | Arabic | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `2+1+1` | - | the weekly hours split into sessions: one double + two singles |
| `(3)`, `(4)` | حصّة أسبوعيّة بنظام الأفواج | **weekly session run in GROUPS** - the class splits |
| `①`, `②` circled | حصّة نصف شهريّة لكامل الفصل | **fortnightly** session, whole class (Week A / Week B) |
| `△ 1/2` triangle | حصّة لكامل الفصل بالتّداول الأسبوعي بين ساعة وساعتين | whole class, **alternating weekly between 1 hour and 2 hours** |
| `△ 1/(4)` triangle | حصّة بالتّداول الأسبوعي بين ساعة واحدة لكامل الفصل و4 ساعات بنظام الأفواج (ساعتان لكلّ فوج) | alternates weekly: **week A = 1h whole class, week B = 4h in groups (2h per group)**. The two must be placed in **non-simultaneous slots** across weeks A and B |
| `(2)` circled | حصّة نصف شهريّة لكامل الفصل بنظام الأفواج (ساعة لكلّ فوج في نفس اليوم) | fortnightly, in groups, **1 hour per group on the SAME DAY** |
| `(4)` circled | حصّة نصف شهريّة بنظام الأفواج (ساعتان لكلّ فوج كلّ نصف شهر) | fortnightly, in groups, **2 hours per group** |
| `(4)` boxed | حصّة أسبوعيّة بنظام الأفواج تخصّ المعاهد النّموذجيّة | weekly group session, **pilot institutes only** |

## Why this matters more than anything else so far

**Pupil hours and teacher hours are different numbers.** The Sport-stream table
gives three columns per year: توقيت التلميذ (pupil), توقيت الأستاذ (teacher),
الضارب (coefficient). For Biology in that stream: **pupil 3, teacher 5.**

The footnote explains it: `(ح) ساعة كامل الفصل وساعتين بنظام الأفواج` - one hour
whole class plus two hours in groups. The pupil experiences 1 + 2 = 3 hours.
The teacher works 1 + (2 x 2 groups) = 5 hours.

Likewise `(م) بنظام الأفواج` for Computer Science: pupil 1, teacher 2.

**This is the formal version of what Majd described** as "a 3-hour session is
really 1.5 hours for two groups". Our current model has one `hours` number per
class+subject and would get teacher workload wrong wherever groups appear.
The Curriculum sheet needs to carry both, or carry the group pattern and derive
them.

# I - RULES FOR THE PUPIL / CLASS (توصيات تهمّ التّلميذ / القسم)

1. **One teacher per class per subject.** يُسند القسم الواحد في كلّ مادّة إلى مدرّس واحد
2. **Max 6 hours a day; minimum 2 hours in any morning or evening.**
   *Explicitly does NOT apply to Physical Education and optional subjects.*
3. **A 2-hour separation between the morning and evening sessions**, whatever
   school time system is used. يتمّ الفصل بين الحصص الصّباحيّة والمسائيّة بساعتين
   → **CONFIRMED: the school does have this break.** Periods 5-6 (12:00-14:00)
   in `config.json` are closed every day. That leaves 4 morning + 4 evening
   teaching periods, which is exactly the circular's "max 4 hours for a pupil
   in one session".
4. **Limit room changes** for a class within one session, except subjects that
   require specialised rooms.
5. **Five consecutive study hours are allowed only in the first four days** of
   the week, and only if rule 3 is still respected.
6. **BAC pupils must get at least one free afternoon in the first four days of
   the week.** ينبغي منح تلاميذ البكالوريا أمسية راحة على الأقلّ في الأيّام الأربعة الأولى
7. **Avoid hollow hours.** ينبغي تجنّب السّاعات الجوفاء *(bold in the original)*
8. Balance and homogeneity when distributing pupils between classes and between
   the two groups of one class.

# II - RULES FOR THE TEACHER (توصيات تهمّ المدرّس)

1. Respect the days set aside for pedagogical training.
2. **Weekly hours balanced across working days**, excluding the training day.
   **Max 6 teaching hours a day, minimum 2 in any morning or evening.** Hours
   actually taught are what count.
3. A teacher **may** be given five consecutive teaching hours on **Friday and
   Saturday**.
4. **Alternation (المراوحة) between morning and evening sessions**, for every
   teacher, in every subject, during the **first four days** of the week.
5. A teacher is given **two levels**, or two tracks, or two streams with
   different programmes.
6. Ensure each teacher's required hours are met; extra hours possible if needed.

# III - RULES FOR THE LEARNING (توصيات تهمّ التّعلّمات)

1. A subject's weekly hours are spread across **both morning and evening**
   periods, whatever the subject. *Explicitly does NOT apply to BAC classes.*
2. Within one session, lessons are programmed to vary the learning:
   - **1st year secondary:** **three quarters** of the hours of each core
     subject (**Arabic, French, Maths**) in the **morning**, balanced across
     the week; the remaining quarter in the evening.
   - **2nd, 3rd, 4th year:** **three quarters** of each **stream-specific**
     subject in the **morning**, balanced across the week; the quarter in the
     evening.
   - **Physical Education:** either one 2-hour session, or two separated
     1-hour sessions - **except BAC classes, which get a single 2-hour
     session.** *Always keep **24 hours** between the two PE sessions.*
   - Subjects taught **2 hours a week must not fall on two consecutive days**.
3. Coordinate the subjects that use the **group system** with those using
   **fortnightly** sessions.
4. Practical work happens in **specialised rooms and nowhere else**, fully
   utilised. Ordinary rooms only in **extreme necessity** (not enough
   specialised rooms), and specialised rooms must not be given over to ordinary
   subjects.

## Majd's BAC priority is NOT subjective

Majd raised final-year priority and worried it would look self-interested
(he studies bac maths). The circular independently requires it, three times:

- **I.6** - bac pupils get a free afternoon in the first four days
- **III.1** - the morning/evening spreading rule is **waived for bac classes**
- **III.2** - bac PE is a single 2-hour session, unlike other years

So bac priority is ministry policy, not a favour. What is **not** in the
circular is Majd's additional request to avoid **Friday evening** for bac. The
earlier inspectorate text says to free an evening for final classes *"غير
أمسيات الجمعة والسبت"* - not Friday or Saturday evenings - which concerns
*which* evening is freed, not a ban on Friday teaching. **Marked as a local
preference, not a ministry rule, until Majd confirms.**
